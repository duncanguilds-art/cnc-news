CNC Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers CNC Uploader, a private tool used only by the owner of the CNC YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, CNC Uploader asks for permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to upload CNC's own videos and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing settings.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is saved only on the owner's own computer. CNC Uploader does not collect, store or process information about anyone other than the channel owner, and it has no server or database of its own.

Sharing

CNC Uploader does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. Data goes only between the owner's computer and Google's YouTube API.

Google and YouTube

CNC Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the saved token stop working; the owner can also delete it from their computer.
