CNC — Clanker News Channel

CNC is a YouTube channel where two animated robot anchors, Lug and Ratchet, cover recent science and history news. Every story is researched from the original publishers and papers, and every source is credited on screen and in the video description.

CNC Uploader

CNC Uploader is a private tool that the channel's owner runs on their own computer to upload CNC's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the CNC YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

CNC Uploader uses YouTube API Services. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
